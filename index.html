❓ Quick Answers to Common Questions

🖥️ Do I need to install extra drivers?

The app works with standard Android USB protocols. You don't need to install special drivers for your PC to install the app itself — just allow USB debugging in developer options on your phone, if prompted.

📱 Will this drain my phone's battery fast?

Streaming video does use battery, but your phone stays partially charged via the USB connection from the goggles, giving you extended flying sessions without worrying about a dead phone.

🔗 Can I view the video on a computer, not just a phone?

Absolutely! Once you use openfpvcast's WebRTC broadcasting feature, any device on your WiFi network with a web browser can view the live feed. This works great on laptops, smart TVs, or even another phone.

🎥 Can I record audio along with video?

Yes, you can optionally enable your phone's microphone during recording. This adds your voice or ambient sounds to the video file, perfect for reviews or live commentary.
